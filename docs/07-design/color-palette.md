## 1. Mau Sac Nhan Dien Thuong Hieu (Brand Colors)

| Ten Token | Gia tri Hex | CSS Variable | Muc dich su dung |

## 2. Mau Sac Canh Bao & Trang Thai (Semantic Colors)

| Trang thai | Gia tri Hex | Mau nen nhat (10%) | Y nghia trong he thong |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Success (Thanh cong)** | `#10B981` | `#ECFDF5` | Ung vien duoc tuyen (`HIRED`), phong van thanh cong, bang luong da duyet. |
| **Warning (Canh bao)** | `#F59E0B` | `#FFFBEB` | Ho so dang cho kiem duyet, cham cong di muon, ca dem `is_overnight`. |
| **Danger / Error (Loi)** | `#EF4444` | `#FEF2F2` | Ung vien bi tu choi (`REJECTED`), phong van that bai, vi pham giam sat proctoring. |
| **Info (Thong tin)** | `#3B82F6` | `#EFF6FF` | Tin dang tuyen dung moi, thong bao he thong. |

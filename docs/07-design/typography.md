| Level | Size (rem / px) | Line Height | Weight | Ung dung tren giao dien |

## 3. Quy Tac Trinh Bay Chu (Typographic Rules)

1. **Tuyet doi khong dung em dash (-)** trong toan bo he thong van ban san pham va tai lieu (Theo quy dinh tai `10-ai/rules-and-constraints.md`). Thay vao do, su dung dau gach noi don (`-`) hoac dau hai cham (`:`).
2. **Khong be dong tuy tien**: Tranh de tu lech loi don doc mot minh o cuoi dong (widow/orphan words).
3. **Do dai dong toi uu**: Doan van ban mo ta cong viec nen co do dai ly tuong tu 60 - 80 ky tu tren mot dong de dam bao do tap trung va de doc.

## 1. Vong Doi Cua Mot Ban Ghi ADR

- **PROPOSED**: Dang trong qua trinh thao luan, chua co su thong nhat cuoi cung.
- **ACCEPTED**: Da duoc phe duyet boi Tech Lead / Architecture Team va dang duoc thuc thi.
- **DEPRECATED**: Khong con ap dung trong he thong.
- **SUPERSEDED**: Da duoc thay the boi mot ADR moi hon.
